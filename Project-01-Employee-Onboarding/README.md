# 👨‍💼 Project 1 – Employee Onboarding Automation

## 📌 Overview

This project automates the employee onboarding process by collecting employee information, storing it in Google Sheets, and sending a welcome email automatically.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Many HR teams manually:

- Collect employee information
- Enter data into spreadsheets
- Send welcome emails
- Track new employees

This repetitive process is slow and increases the risk of human error.

---

# 💡 Solution

I designed an automation using Zapier that:

1. Collects employee information through a Google Form.
2. Saves the information to Google Sheets.
3. Sends an automated welcome email to the new employee.

---

⬇️

⬇️
